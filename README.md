# AtliQ Hardwares Sales Analytics — Excel Project

## 📊 Project Overview

This project is an **Excel-based Sales Analytics report for AtliQ Hardwares**. The analysis focuses on understanding sales performance across customers, countries, products, and business divisions using historical sales data.

The report analyzes sales for **2019, 2020, and 2021**, with values presented in **USD**. It also compares 2021 performance against targets and highlights top and bottom performing products and markets.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

- Analyze customer-wise net sales performance.
- Compare sales performance across 2019, 2020, and 2021.
- Measure year-over-year sales growth.
- Compare 2021 actual sales against targets.
- Identify the top 10 products based on sales performance.
- Analyze sales performance at the division level.
- Identify the top 5 and bottom 5 products based on quantity sold.
- Identify new products introduced in 2021 and their sales contribution.
- Identify the top 5 countries based on 2021 sales.

## 📈 Key Insights

- AtliQ Hardwares' overall net sales increased from **$196.7M in 2020 to $598.9M in 2021**.
- Customer net sales increased by **304.5%** from 2020 to 2021.
- India was the largest market in 2021 with **$161.3M** in sales.
- The USA was the second-largest market among the countries shown, with **$87.8M** in 2021 sales.
- The **PC division** recorded the highest percentage growth among the three divisions, at **313.7%**.
- The listed top 10 products increased from **$6.4M in 2020 to $52.0M in 2021**.
- The 2021 market performance was **9.2% below the overall target**.
- The listed new products launched in 2021 generated **$176.2M** in sales.

# AtliQ Hardwares Sales Analytics — Excel Project

## 📊 Project Overview

This project presents an **Excel-based Sales Analytics report for AtliQ Hardwares**, focused on analyzing sales performance across customers, markets, products, and divisions.

The analysis covers **2019, 2020, and 2021 sales data**, with values presented in **USD**. The report includes customer-wise sales performance, market performance against targets, top products, division-level performance, product quantity rankings, and new-product performance.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze customer-wise net sales performance.
- Compare sales across 2019, 2020, and 2021.
- Calculate and analyze year-over-year sales growth.
- Compare 2021 sales against market targets.
- Identify the top 10 products based on sales.
- Analyze sales performance by division.
- Identify the top 5 and bottom 5 products based on quantity sold.
- Analyze products introduced in 2021 and their sales.
- Identify the top 5 countries based on 2021 sales.

## 📈 Key Insights

- Total customer net sales increased from **$196.7M in 2020 to $598.9M in 2021**, with a reported **304.5%** increase.
- The overall 2021 market performance was **$54.9M below target**, corresponding to **-9.2%**.
- **India** recorded the highest 2021 sales among the countries shown, at **$161.3M**.
- The **PC division** had the highest reported year-over-year growth among the divisions at **313.7%**.
- The Top 10 Products increased from **$6.4M in 2020 to $52.0M in 2021**, with reported growth of **708.0%**.
- The products listed in the New Products-2021 report generated a combined **$176.2M** in 2021 sales.
- The Top 5 Countries report shows combined sales of **$364.9M** in 2021.
